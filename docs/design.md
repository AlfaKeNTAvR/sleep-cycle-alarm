# Design

Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/9caJhL63zpQnX9oeweSbpV
Final screens: "Before bed" plus the "with cycles" row (B2, C2, D2), as edited by the user on 2026-09-17. The first night-screen row is kept only for comparison.

Look: dark, low-glare (used at bedtime). Fraunces for large numbers, Manrope for text, warm amber accent.

## Before bed

- Band connection status.
- "Wake me by" deadline with on/off switch.
- "Sleep up to": 4.5 / 6 / 7.5 / 9 h. Options that cannot fit before the deadline (from now plus an estimated time to fall asleep) are disabled and covered with thin "/" lines at 45 degrees, about 1 mm apart, same stroke weight as the text. No warning text.
- **Live volume line (owner spec, 2026-10-22, PER-133, option C "quiet text line"):** directly above Start night, one centred line naming where media plays and its volume: an output icon (16dp, stroke style like the app's other icons), a 6dp gap, then "Speaker 52%", "Headphones 16%" or "Bluetooth 40%" in 14sp muted text with the percent bold in the main text color. A reference for picking the fade's starting volume ("so I know as a reference which sound I like"), since speaker and headphones differ. The percent is the media stream volume as a percent of its range, the same number the SIMULATED block shows, re-read every 500 ms, so a key press, the bedtime fade's own steps and a switch of output all show within half a second. The built-in speaker reads Speaker, a wired or USB headset Headphones, Bluetooth A2DP or a BLE headset or speaker Bluetooth, anything else Speaker. No "fading to" text (owner, 2026-10-22). Hidden while the amber SIMULATED block is up, which already shows the volume, so it never shows twice.
- Start night.

There is no phone-backup switch: the phone alarm is the only alarm there is, deadline or not, so there is nothing to opt into.

## Night screen

One screen whose content depends on the moment you look at it. Opening it syncs first.

- **A live night (N1, decided 2026-09-21 - one layout, replacing the separate A, B and C states below):** three centred lines and nothing else. Which alarm is coming, in plain text ("Morning alarm" / "Nap alarm" / "Out-of-bed nudge", or "No alarm armed" when nothing is - a nap at or after the morning alarm's time is the morning alarm, owner decision 2026-10-03, and is named and rung as one); its own time as the big amber numeral; and how long until then ("in 20 min", "in 5 h 38"). Same layout going to bed, asleep, awake again and napping - the mode changes the words in those three lines, never their number or arrangement. When the morning alarm has already rung while the band still reads asleep, there is no next alarm, so the numeral is a dash and the line underneath reads "Morning alarm rang at 07:00" instead of a countdown.
- **What A, B and C used to add, all removed by N1, none of it moved elsewhere:** the onset captions ("If you fall asleep by 00:25", "Asleep since 01:12", "Napping, asleep since 07:06"), "7.5 h of sleep", "Deadline HH:mm", "Alarm rings at the deadline", "You slept 1 h 59" as the hero number, "Fall back asleep by 02:45", "No full cycle fits before 08:30", the nap explainer card, the tonight-so-far row, and the engine's own one-sentence reason under the mode header. The owner's verdict on the result: "way too much information". Total slept is on the morning report, which is where he reads it. (An earlier owner request had already removed the "possible wake-ups" timeline these states carried, for the same reason: not worth reading at 3am.)
- **Night finished (the FINISHED amendment):** the deadline passed and the morning alarm has rung (or the deadline is more than an hour behind - owner decision, 2026-10-03: a deadline that passes with nothing rung, the phone off or its alarms wiped, rings 2 min after the app next runs instead of ending the night in silence), or the two nap alarms the whole night allows (pre-wake or after the wake alarm alike) are both spent with no deadline left to fall back on - not band-detected wake by itself any more. Reason text plus an "End night" button; the alarm is not re-armed from here, it already rang. If the two naps are spent but a deadline is still ahead, the screen does not reach this state at all: it stays on the ordinary live-night layout, with the alarm held at the deadline. **N2 (owner-reported, 2026-09-21): this state is only ever shown when nothing at all is armed.** A finished plan whose out-of-bed nudge is still pending (L2 keeps the chain alive past the deadline) is drawn as the ordinary live-night layout naming that nudge, with Nap and "End night" underneath - saying "Night finished" over a still-armed alarm read as the deadline having cancelled it, which it never does.
- **Band not syncing (P1, owner decision 2026-09-30, "warn, still arm"):** while the last sync failed or returned only stale data, a red banner under the sync line reads "Band not syncing - alarm is only an estimate. Disconnect and reconnect the band in Gadgetbridge." The night keeps running and the estimated alarm stays armed as a backup; the banner disappears with the first sync that succeeds. On 2026-09-30 every sync timed out all night and only the small status line said so; Gadgetbridge showed the band connected (with no battery level), and reconnecting it was the fix.
- **After an alarm rings (P3, owner spec, 2026-09-30).** Once the ring is stopped (or stops itself), the screen shows the out-of-bed nudge in the N1 layout - "Out-of-bed nudge", its time big, "in 10 min" - and at the bottom two stacked buttons: a filled amber **Nap for 20 min**, then the outlined **End night** (it read "I'm up, end night" until 2026-10-02). Pressing Nap swaps the nudge for a nap alarm 20 min from the press (never later than a wake-by deadline still ahead) and the screen then reads "Nap alarm", its time, "in 20 min", with only **I'm up** underneath (one choice at a time, below). When that nap rings and is stopped, the nudge and the Nap button come back - as many times as he likes, no cap. Nothing here watches the band: he says when he naps. The alarm block stays centred on the screen exactly where it sits on every other Night screen; the buttons never push it up. Pressing Nap never ends the night; only **End night** does.
- **One choice at a time (owner spec, 2026-10-02).** The live-night buttons depend on whether he is asleep as far as the app knows. While the morning alarm is still ahead, or his own nap is, the only button is **I'm up** (behind an "Are you up?" confirmation): there is no End night before the morning alarm. I'm up counts as the morning alarm being stopped now (or, during his own nap, replaces the nap): the phone alarm is cancelled and the out-of-bed nudge is armed 10 min out, which brings **Nap for 20 min** and **End night**. Once he is up (the nudge ahead, or the night over), the screen offers Nap (while a nudge is pending) and **End night**, never I'm up. So abandoning a night at 3 am takes I'm up, then End night.
- **A night under 1 hour is discarded (owner spec, 2026-10-21, PER-126).** "If the sleep was shorter than 1 hour, then it shouldn't ask me any questions... doesn't show me the final screen... and doesn't save the log because it doesn't matter unless it was a simulation." Less than 1 h after Start night (counted on the night's own clock: the wall clock, or the simulated clock in a simulation; not from falling asleep), both End night and I'm up open the same confirmation instead of their usual one: "Discard this night?" with "It is under 1 hour, so nothing is saved." under it, and Cancel / Discard. A tap outside or Cancel does nothing. Discard cancels every alarm of the night (the morning alarm, the nudge, his own nap), stops the tracking, puts his own media volume back as End night does, and goes straight back to Before bed: no morning report, no rating card, no 15:00 question, and the night is not in Logs. I'm up here discards rather than arming the nudge. A simulated night keeps its log (closed with `night_discarded`, not `night_end`, so it is never asked about later) and otherwise behaves the same. At 1 h or more nothing changes.
- **Live volume line (owner spec, 2026-10-22, PER-133):** the same line as on Before bed, directly above the buttons on every live night state: the morning alarm ahead with I'm up, the out-of-bed nudge with Nap for 20 min and End night, and his own nap with I'm up (and a finished night with End night). Not on the morning report, Past night, Logs, Settings or dialogs, and hidden on a simulated night, whose amber block already shows the volume.
- **D. Morning:** total slept, each stretch with from-to times, length and cycles in parentheses (one decimal), note that the night log was saved. A night with no stretch at all says "No sleep recorded tonight" - unless its syncs were failing when it ended (P1), when it says "No band data reached the app: the band's syncs were failing", because the app never saw the band's data and cannot claim there was no sleep.
- **D. Morning, rating (owner spec, 2026-10-02):** under the stretches, a "How did you sleep?" card with three faces, Good / Okay / Bad in green #7BC89A, amber #F0B75A and red #E0806E. Optional: Done without a tap skips it; tapping another face changes it. It replaces the "night log saved" note, which comes back when the rating is switched off or the night was started before the rating existed.
- **Why Okay or Bad (owner spec, 2026-10-04):** picking Okay or Bad opens a dialog of tappable tiles, an outline icon with its name under it, any number ticked. Morning, "What felt off?": Still sleepy, Headache, Woke before alarm, Slow to fall asleep, Kept napping (three over two, Still sleepy and Headache first since 2026-10-05; Groggy on waking and Heavy, overslept were dropped by the owner on 2026-10-04). 15:00, "How is your afternoon?": Sleepy in afternoon, Low energy, Hard to focus, Heavy head (two by two). Owner change, 2026-10-21: Kept napping (after the alarm, kept moving it or tapping Nap to sleep more) and Heavy head added, each last in its list; their icons (a round-again arrow around a z, and the Headache head with a sinking arrow) were drawn in the same outline style, not taken from an approved design. Subtitle "You picked Okay. Tick all that apply." Dialog #181C24, radius 28; tiles at least 104dp tall, radius 16, 2dp border #3A404D; a ticked tile takes the rating's color for border, icon and name over a 13% tint of it. "Save" ("Save (2)" once two are ticked) stores the set; "Skip" (or a tap outside) writes nothing. Good never asks. Owner change, 2026-10-05: every Okay or Bad pick asks again, Okay to Bad and the same rating again included, prefilled with the ticks; only Good clears them. Under the faces, the ticked symptoms as small icons with names, tappable to change them; nothing shows when none are ticked.

## Settings (owner spec, 2026-10-02, option A)

Section titles, no descriptions. Every change is saved at once.

- **After the alarm:** Out-of-bed nudge, 5, 10 or 15 min (default 10), measured from the ring's end. Nap length, 10 to 30 min in 5 min steps (default 20), used by both the Nap button and the engine's naps between cycles. Both apply from the next Start night.
- **Bedtime audio:** the section header names the output whose volumes are shown: "BEDTIME AUDIO (PHONE)" or "BEDTIME AUDIO (BLUETOOTH)" (wired, USB and anything else count as Phone), updating live with the output in use (owner spec, 2026-10-22, PER-135). Rows: Pause media when asleep (on/off), Fade media volume (on/off), Starting volume and Ending volume for that output, and one shared Fade duration, 5 to 60 min in 5 min steps (default 15 min). Each output remembers its own starting and ending volume ("remembered per device and automatically switched depending on whether I'm listening from the speaker" or Bluetooth); changing them changes only the output in use. They step through the phone's own media volume steps, shown as the same percent the live volume line shows (4% apart on the Pixel's 25 steps: starting 12% to 52%, default 24%; ending 4% to 44%, default 4%; owner, 2026-10-22: "do the same levels as the phone"). A value saved before (25% and 5%) reads as its nearest step, a tie going up. At or above the starting volume the ending volume just holds it. The fade begins once media is playing after Start night AND the screen is off (owner, 2026-10-21: "I don't want it to start dropping the audio while the screen is still active"; always-on display counts as off), within about 1 s (the night service watches playback, the media volume and the screen, and also checks on every sync), and again each time you wake in the night with media playing (owner request, 2026-10-02). It sets the output's starting volume exactly, even when that raises it, and then fades smoothly (PER-134): the loudness falls in dB at a steady speed worked out from that output's starting and ending volume and the duration, the hardware volume stepping down one step at a time and a small digital gain filling the gap between steps, updated 5 times a second only while the fade is falling. If the screen comes on mid-fade the fade freezes and carries on from there once it is off again. A volume you raise or lower yourself (side buttons too) becomes the fade's new start and keeps falling at the same speed, so a raise just takes longer; nothing is pulled back. If media moves to or from Bluetooth mid-fade, that output's own pair applies from then on, continuing from that output's own volume. If the digital gain cannot be used (another effects app, an output that bypasses it) the fade follows the same line in plain volume steps, noted once in the night log. At the ending volume it holds. Falling asleep pauses the media and parks the volume at the starting volume (or where the fade already got to, if lower; turning it up while parked is pulled back to the starting volume). Once the morning alarm has rung there is no fading: the next sync puts your own volume back (End night does too, for a night that ends before its alarm, and so does a night that finishes on its own or is discarded). The digital gain is released whenever a fade ends, parks, is switched off or reaches its ending volume, and before any alarm sound starts. Pressing play while the band still says asleep (it confirms an awakening late, or never if you lie still) starts a fresh fade too; if the band still says asleep once that fade has reached the ending volume and the fade duration has passed, the media is paused and the volume parked again (owner decision, 2026-10-03). The band reports awakenings minutes late, so this pause can land while you are already awake and listening; that risk was accepted on 2026-10-03. A fade setting changed mid-night applies at once (owner decision, 2026-10-03): a new starting volume at the next fade, a new ending volume or duration to the running fade, and the fade switched off starts no new fade (a running one finishes in plain steps). A sync is booked for the moment the running fade will have run its course, so the pause after it is decided on time while asleep.
- **Sleep rating:** Rate the night (on/off), Ask again later (on/off), Ask at (default 15:00).
- **Debug** (debug builds only): Simulated band data (on/off), Simulated start (default 23:00), Ring phone alarm. Switching simulated band data on starts the simulated clock at the next Simulated start (today's if still ahead, else tomorrow's), at 1x. The time is kept until changed: End night and the 2 h idle reset turn the switch off but leave it (owner request, 2026-10-03).
- **More:** Setup, Open Gadgetbridge (owner request, 2026-10-05: opens Gadgetbridge to reconnect the band). (Debug is its own section just above More, in debug builds only.)

A row whose switch above it is off stays visible, dimmed and inert. The fade needs the pause: with "Pause media when asleep" off, the fade rows dim and the fade switch reads off.

## Later rating notification

At the "Ask at" time on the day the night ended, a notification: "Still feel the same about last night?" with "This morning you said Good." - or "How do you feel about last night?" when the morning rating was skipped. Three buttons, Good / Okay / Bad. Good rates without opening the app; Okay and Bad (owner spec, 2026-10-04) rate and open the app on that night's "How is your afternoon?" dialog. Owner spec, 2026-10-21 (PER-124): Okay and Bad open that night's Past night screen with the dialog on top, prefilled with any ticks already given, and a tap on the notification itself opens that night's Past night without the dialog ("if I click good, it's fine, it just resolves it. But if I click anything else, it should open the log for me, so I can pick the symptoms there."). This holds whether the app was closed or already open on another screen. Back from that Past night goes to the Logs list, and Back again to Before bed (or the Night screen while a night runs), as from any Past night. Asked once per night; not asked when the night ended after that time or the time already passed.

## Alarm ring screen

Shown full-screen, over the lock screen, whichever alarm just rang (the main wake alarm, a nap alarm - the owner's own "Nap for 20 min" included - or the out-of-bed nudge). One button:

- **Stop:** silences the sound and vibration only. The night keeps running, and the out-of-bed nudge rings 10 min after the Stop (P3; 10 min after the ring's own 9 min auto-stop if nobody touches it). Napping is chosen on the Night screen, not here: N1 keeps this screen to the one button. (Before P3: "falling back asleep afterward still counts as a nap", detected by the band - gone.)

N1 (decided 2026-09-21) removed the second button, **I'm awake**, which silenced the ring and ended the night on the spot. A mis-tap half asleep therefore cancelled every alarm left in the night, the repeating out-of-bed nudge included. Ending a night now takes the Night screen's own button, behind its own confirmation, which is deliberately harder to reach without being awake.

The out-of-bed nudge is the same screen with different wording ("Time to get up" instead of "Wake up"); Stop behaves identically either way.

## Logs

The night history, not just an export list. Saved nights newest first by when they started, each row showing the night's start date and time (owner request, 2026-10-02: not when its log was last written), its size, and a "simulated" tag where it applies.

- **Tapping a row** reopens that night as its own screen (below).
- **The three-dot button on the right** of each row opens a menu: Share (the system share sheet) and Delete (one confirmation, then the file is gone). Chosen over long-press and over swipe-to-delete because it is the only one of the three you can see rather than have to guess.

Rating chips (owner spec, 2026-10-02): after the size, one small colored chip per rating the night has - the one after End night first, then the later one. Nights from before the rating have none.

## Past night

A "Your rating" card (owner spec, 2026-10-02) between the report and the Wake by card: two rows, "After End night, 07:52" and "Later in the day, 15:02", each three pill buttons that change that rating. A night from before the rating has no card. Under an Okay or Bad row, the symptoms ticked for it as small icons with names (nothing when none are ticked); tapping that line opens the same dialog to edit them any time (owner spec, 2026-10-04). Changing a rating to Good clears that moment's symptoms; picking Okay or Bad, from anything, asks again with the ticks kept (2026-10-05).

The same retrospective view D shows, for a night that ended earlier: total slept under "You slept", each stretch with from-to times, length and cycles, and below it that night's deadline ("Wake by") and picked length.

Nights logged before the app recorded the per-stretch detail cannot draw that card. They show the real total with one honest line saying how many stretches there were but not when - never invented or zeroed times. A night that was never ended has no summary at all, and says so. A night that ended with no band data (P1, flagged in its `night_end`) says so with D's same no-band-data wording; a log written before that flag existed keeps "No sleep recorded tonight".
